## Concern 1 — Result transparency · 关切一 —— 赛果透明度

**Documented facts (organizers' own materials and the public project wall) · 有据可查的事实（主办方自有材料与公开的项目墙）：**

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| • The published rules state the **community vote is worth 30%** of the total score. *(evidence/01-scoring-rules)* | • 公布的规则写明**社区投票占总分 30%**。*（evidence/01-scoring-rules）* |
| • In the announced Top-20, the **No. 1** project displayed, on the public wall, **~10 community votes and 0 GitHub stars** at capture time. *(evidence/02-rankings)* | • 在公布的 Top 20 中，排在**第 1 名**的项目，截图时在项目墙上显示约 **10 个社区投票、0 个 GitHub star**。*（evidence/02-rankings）* |
| • Other projects displayed **hundreds of votes** and, in some cases, **2,000+ stars**. *(evidence/02-rankings)* | • 其他项目显示有**数百个投票**，部分项目有 **2000+ 个 star**。*（evidence/02-rankings）* |
| • Asked in public to explain the ranking against the 30% rule, the organizers **declined to publish a score breakdown.** *(evidence/04-organizer-responses)* | • 当有人在公开场合请求依据 30% 规则解释该排名时，主办方**拒绝公开评分明细**。*（evidence/04-organizer-responses）* |

**Context, fairness, and questions · 背景、公允说明与问题：**

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| **A fair acknowledgement.** Community vote is only 30%. A project could in principle rank highly on the AI-evaluation and judge components (the other 70%) despite low public votes. **That is exactly why the itemized breakdown matters:** with per-component scores published, an unusual result is either explained or it isn't. | **一点公允的说明。** 社区投票只占 30%。一个项目即便公开票数很低，也可能凭借 AI 评估与评委两项（共 70%）获得高分。**而这恰恰说明了为何逐项明细至关重要：** 一旦公开各分项得分，看似异常的结果要么得到解释，要么没有。 |
| **This is not a complaint about the top-ranked team.** They may well have earned strong AI and judge scores. The question is directed at the **committee's transparency**, not at any competitor. | **这不是针对第 1 名团队的指控。** 他们的 AI 与评委得分很可能确实优秀。问题指向的是**组委会的透明度**，而非任何竞争对手。 |
| **Open questions:** (1) What were the per-component scores (AI, vote, judge) for each finalist? (2) How was the 30% weight applied to a ~10-vote project vs. ones with hundreds? (3) Will the full itemized table be published for all projects? | **待答问题：** (1) 各入围项目的分项得分（AI、投票、评委）分别是多少？(2) 30% 的投票权重，如何套用在约 10 票的项目与数百票的项目之间？(3) 是否会公开所有项目完整的逐项评分表？ |

## Concern 2 — Apparent exposure of personal data · 关切二 —— 疑似泄露个人数据

**Documented facts · 有据可查的事实：**

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| • A backend endpoint of the form **`GET /api/teams?eventId=...`** returned team data **including plaintext email addresses and other personal fields**, reportedly covering **all 144 participating teams**. *(evidence/03-data-exposure — all personal data redacted)* | • 一个形如 **`GET /api/teams?eventId=...`** 的后端接口，返回了团队数据，**其中包含明文电子邮箱地址及其他个人字段**，据称覆盖**全部 144 支参赛团队**。*（evidence/03-data-exposure——个人数据已全部打码）* |
| • After this was raised, an organizer publicly characterized accessing the endpoint as **"malicious scraping,"** rather than acknowledging the exposure. *(evidence/04-organizer-responses)* | • 在此问题被提出后，一名主办方在公开场合将访问该接口的行为称为**"恶意爬取"**，而非承认存在数据暴露。*（evidence/04-organizer-responses）* |

**Why it matters & responsible disclosure · 为何重要与负责任的披露：**

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| **Factual context, not a legal conclusion.** Under Singapore's **PDPA**, organizations must make reasonable security arrangements. A breach is **notifiable to the PDPC** if it is likely to cause **significant harm**, **or** affects **500+ individuals**, within **3 calendar days** of assessing it as notifiable. Even below 500, the **significant-harm** limb can apply (e.g. exposed contacts enabling phishing). | **事实背景，非法律结论。** 根据新加坡**《个人数据保护法》(PDPA)**，机构须采取合理的安全保护措施。当泄露**可能造成重大伤害**，**或**影响 **500 人及以上**时，须在评估为应通报后 **3 个自然日内向 PDPC 通报**。即便不足 500 人，**"重大伤害"**这一情形仍可能适用（例如被暴露的联系方式可用于钓鱼）。 |
| **Responsible disclosure.** This repository **does not** republish the exposed data — doing so would harm the affected participants. The evidence shows only that the exposure existed (endpoint shape, field names, counts), with every value blacked out. | **负责任的披露。** 本仓库**不**重新公开被暴露的数据——那样做会伤害受影响的参赛者。证据仅表明暴露曾存在（接口形态、字段名、数量），所有取值均已涂黑。 |
| **Open questions:** (1) Has the endpoint been secured, and for what period was it accessible? (2) Have affected participants been notified? (3) Has the PDPC been notified; if not, on what basis was it assessed non-notifiable? | **待答问题：** (1) 接口是否已修复？可被访问的起止时间是？(2) 是否已通知受影响的参赛者？(3) 是否已通报 PDPC？若未通报，依据什么判断无需通报？ |

## Concern 3 — Handling of questions · 关切三 —— 对提问的处理

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| **Documented fact:** When participants asked, in the public channel, for an explanation of the ranking, the organizers asked to move to **"1v1 private calls"** and stated they **would not provide score breakdowns.** *(evidence/04-organizer-responses)* | **有据可查的事实：** 当参赛者在公开频道请求解释排名时，主办方要求转入**"1 对 1 私聊"**，并表示**不会提供评分明细**。*（evidence/04-organizer-responses）* |
| **Why it matters (opinion).** A public competition with public rules and a public vote invites public accountability. Routing every question into private channels, while declining to publish the data that would settle them, is — in the author's view — the opposite of the transparency the format promises. The remedy is the same in every case: **publish the breakdown.** | **为何重要（观点）。** 一场规则公开、投票公开的公开赛事，理应接受公开问责。把所有问题都引导进私下渠道，同时拒绝公开能解答这些问题的数据——在作者看来——与这种赛制承诺的透明恰好相反。而补救之道始终一样：**公开明细。** |
| **Open question:** Will the committee respond **on the record**, in the same public channel where the rules and results were announced? | **待答问题：** 组委会是否会在公布规则与赛果的**同一个公开频道**里，**公开、留痕地**回应？ |

## What we are asking for · 我们的诉求

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| 1. **Publish the full, itemized scoring table** — AI evaluation, community vote, judge — for all finalists, ideally all projects. | 1. **公开完整、逐项的评分表**——AI 评估、社区投票、评委——至少覆盖全部入围项目，最好覆盖全部项目。 |
| 2. **Acknowledge and remediate the data exposure:** confirm it is fixed, notify affected participants, and confirm whether the PDPC was notified. | 2. **承认并修复数据暴露：** 确认已修复，通知受影响的参赛者，并说明是否已通报 PDPC。 |
| 3. **Answer on the record,** in public, rather than only in private calls. | 3. **公开、留痕地回应**，而非仅以私聊方式回应。 |
